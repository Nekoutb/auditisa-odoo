Working on a working paper

Every standard working paper in AuditISA is laid out the same way. This chapter walks through one example, S4.2 Going Concern, Preliminary (ISA 570). Once you know it, you know them all.

Who: everyone on the engagement team. The quality reviewer can read and raise notes but does not change the work.

Opening the paper

Click Strategy and risks in the top bar, then click S4.2 in the task list. You can also find it under Tasks. The paper opens on one scrolling page in three columns: the step list on the left, the work in the middle, and the tools, attachments and assignment on the right.

The header

  • Title and depth label: the code and name, then a label such as Non-complex · document by exception. Under it you see the client, the year, the standard, the edition (Edition: ISA in force) and the deadline. The deadline turns red when it has passed.
  • Progress: for example "0 of 7 tasks done · not signed". It also counts tasks done in a tool, tasks closed by exception, and open review notes.
  • Prepare / Review: two ways to read the same paper. Prepare shows the tips and the full requirements. Review hides them so the reviewer sees only the work. See Review notes and sign-off.
  • Save the paper: nothing is recorded until you click it. The label beside it reads Unsaved changes, then Saved at and the time. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, AuditISA asks you to Stay or Leave without saving.

The step list

The left column lists 1 Objective, each task under 2 Tasks required (2.1 Financial indicators, 2.2 Operating indicators and so on), and 3 Conclusion and sign-off. Click a line to jump to it. A coloured dot shows each task's state, and a red dot with a number shows open review notes. Under Show only:, click Not done or Open notes to filter the list.

1 Objective

This states in a few lines what the paper must achieve. The standard link (for S4.2, ISA 570 (Revised 2024) ↗) opens the official text in a new tab. Click Full requirements to unfold what the standards ask in more detail, including the OHADA rule on equity below half of the share capital.

2 Tasks required

On S4.2 a card called Pre-filled from the data comes first. It shows figures that AuditISA has read from the trial balance, here the equity, half of the share capital and the result of comparing them. Click Open the Financial analysis to see the detail.

Each task then shows its text, its standard reference on the right (for example ISA 570 A3), and a Tip box you can fold away. To complete a task:

  1. Do the work described.
  2. Click Done and describe in the box what you did and found, with the working-paper reference.
  3. If the task does not apply, click Not applicable. A shaded box opens: the reason is required.
  4. Click Save the paper.

A finished task folds to one line. Click it to open it again. A task marked Done in the tool was completed in a linked tool and needs nothing more on the paper.

How much you write depends on the profile

The engagement profile, which comes from the profile questionnaire, sets how much documentation each paper asks for. This keeps the effort in line with the size and risk of the client.

ProfileWhat the tasks look like
Complex · full documentationOne text box per task. You describe the work and the result in full.
Non-complex · document by exceptionThree buttons per task: No exceptions noted, Exceptions noted and Not applicable. With No exceptions noted, a standard answer is filled in for you and you must give a Source: a tool, a linked task or an attachment such as A1. With Exceptions noted, a box opens to describe the exceptions, their effect and what was done.
Very simple · tick list and summaryOne tick box (or n/a) per task, then a single box, Summary of work performed: what was done, with whom, the documents seen and the result.

When the paper switches back to full documentation

A lighter layout is never applied where it would be unsafe. The paper opens in full documentation, whatever the profile, when:

  • a tool has raised a warning on this paper (for example, equity is below half of the share capital);
  • a conclusion statement is answered No;
  • the paper is one that is never lightened (C1.1, C3.1, C5.5, C5.6 and C5.11).

A line under the objective tells you why. If a task was closed as No exceptions noted before the warning appeared, AuditISA asks you to write the result instead.

Findings and adjustments

On S4.2, record a matter for the file with the Findings tool in the right-hand column. Every finding raised from any paper builds up in that one tool. On the working papers of substantive procedures (in Execution), each finding also appears at the bottom of the paper in two parts: 1. Finding (the issue, and how and where it happened) and 2. Adjustment to book. When you save the adjustment it goes straight to the summary of audit differences. See Findings and the summary of audit differences.

The right-hand column

  • Tools: links to the tools this paper uses. For S4.2 these are Financial analysis, Significant accounts and Findings.
  • Linked tasks: related papers (S4.1, E6.3, C2.2 and others). Click one to open it. Its Back link brings you back here.
  • Assignment: the Preparer, the Reviewer and the Deadline. A change applies at once and can be reversed with Undo. With no date of its own, the task takes its phase's deadline.
  • Task not applicable: give a reason, then click Mark not applicable. Reinstate the task reverses it.
  • Attachments: click Attach a file, or Pick from this engagement to reuse a document already in the file. Each file gets a reference (A1, A2…). Cite it in your text as [A2] or as a Source. Uploading a file with the same name creates a new version. A removed file can be restored for 30 days.
Careful Attaching or removing a file after signing voids the signatures, because the evidence has changed.

Review notes inside a task

Under each task, + Review note on this task opens a small box. Type the point and click Add the note. The note stays inside the task, next to the work it concerns. See Review notes and sign-off.

3 Conclusion and sign-off

  1. Answer each statement Yes or No. A No needs an explanation. Some statements are checked by a tool. If the tool disagrees with your answer, it shows the tool shows otherwise.
  2. On S4.2, fill in Work required at completion (E6.3) where needed.
  3. Write the Conclusion, or click Use the standard wording and adapt it.
  4. Click Save the paper, then Sign as preparer. If something is missing, Still to complete: lists it.
Good to know If a colleague changes the same box while you are typing, your text is kept and theirs is shown underneath. You can choose Use their version, or save to replace it.
