Questions and words used

Short answers to the questions auditors ask most often, followed by the words AuditISA uses on its screens.

Who: everyone.

Frequently asked questions

My review signature has disappeared. Why?

Someone changed the paper after you signed: an answer, or a file attached or removed. Your signature covered the earlier version, so it was voided. You will find it under Voided signatures with the date and reason. Review the change and sign again. See Review notes and sign-off.

Why can't I sign as reviewer?

The most common reasons: you signed the same paper as preparer (no self-review), review notes are still open, the preparer has not signed yet, or your role is below the minimum in the firm's sign-off policy. The message on screen tells you which one applies.

I clicked "No exceptions noted" but it is greyed out.

A tool has raised a warning on this paper, so it must be documented in full. Describe the work and the result in the text box instead.

Why does my paper show a text box per task when the client is non-complex?

The paper went back to full documentation because a tool raised a warning, a conclusion statement is answered No, or the paper is one that is never lightened. A line under the objective tells you which.

I lost my changes.

Nothing is recorded until you click Save the paper. Watch for Unsaved changes next to the button. If you try to leave a paper with unsaved changes, AuditISA asks you first.

Two of us edited the same paper at the same time.

Each person's changes are kept where they do not overlap. If you both changed the same box, your text stays and your colleague's version is shown underneath with Use their version.

Who can clear my review note?

You, as its author, or a manager or above who did not prepare the paper. Anyone on the team can reply.

How do I correct an adjustment that is already posted?

A reviewer clicks Unpost on the summary of audit differences. Change the adjustment on its working paper, then have it posted again. The unposting is logged.

Can I unarchive a file?

No. An archived file stays sealed and read-only. To add something, use Add an addendum on the archive page. See Archiving and next year.

The Roll forward button refuses to start.

A file can be rolled forward only once it is closed: its report issued or the file archived. You also need to be a manager or above on the team.

A file in the new year is marked "Prior year, carried forward".

It was copied from last year. Check that it still holds, then click Mark as reviewed.

Words used in AuditISA

WordMeaning
EngagementOne audit: one client, one financial year, with its own file and team.
ProfileThe result of the profile questionnaire: Complex, Non-complex or Very simple. It sets which tasks the file holds and how much each paper asks you to write.
Task / working paperOne item of the file index (for example S4.2). Its working paper holds the objective, the tasks required, the conclusion and the signatures.
Document by exceptionOn non-complex files, one click (No exceptions noted) plus a source when nothing is wrong. You write text only when exceptions are noted.
SourceWhere the evidence for an answer is: a tool, a linked task or an attachment reference.
Attachment reference (A1, A2…)The number each file gets on a paper. Cite it as [A2].
Review noteA question or point raised on a task or a conclusion. It stays open until cleared, and is never deleted.
Sign-off policy / matrixThe firm's rules on the minimum role that may prepare, review and approve each task. Set under Firm settings.
Voided signatureA signature cancelled automatically because the paper changed after signing.
Quality reviewerThe engagement quality reviewer (ISQM 2). They review independently, raise notes and complete C4.2, but do not change the team's work.
FindingA matter for attention, a control deficiency or a misstatement, recorded in the Findings tool.
AdjustmentThe debit and credit lines that would correct a misstatement, using the seven headings of the financial statements.
Summary of audit differencesThe list of all adjustments (C1.1), compared with materiality (ISA 450).
Post / postedA reviewer's acceptance of an adjustment line. Only posted lines go to the client copy.
Clearly trivialA difference below the trivial threshold. It is kept aside, not added to the total.
Lead scheduleThe summary of an area's accounts from the trial balance, against the prior year, which the working papers start from.
MSPMandatory substantive procedure: it must be performed for the assertion.
ASPAlternative substantive procedure: chosen when the procedures are designed, to respond to the risk.
Completion checklistThe tool checks and thirteen team questions on C6.1. The partner signs it before the report is issued.
Report dateThe date the auditor's report is issued. After it, only assembly work is allowed.
Assembly periodThe sixty days after the report date for completing the final file.
Archive / sealed copyThe locked, read-only file, with a copy whose every file carries a fingerprint (SHA-256).
AddendumSomething added to an archived file, with who, when and why. The original stays untouched.
Legal holdA block on deleting a file, for disputes or inquiries, even after its retention period.
Roll-forwardCreating next year's file from a closed one, carrying setup, answers and evidence as drafts.
Carried forwardA mark on an answer or file that came from last year and still needs updating or reviewing.
