Welcome and finding your way

This chapter shows you what AuditISA does and how its screens are laid out, so that you always know where you are and how to get where you want to go.

Who: everyone in the firm.

What AuditISA does

AuditISA holds your firm's audit files. Each engagement (one client, one financial year) has its own file, built on the International Standards on Auditing (ISA) and on the OHADA rules. The file lists every task of the audit, from accepting the client to archiving the file. You prepare each task, a more senior colleague reviews it, and the partner signs where the firm's rules ask for it. Only the people on an engagement's team can open that engagement: your role in the firm alone does not give you access to a file.

Your home page

When you sign in, AuditISA opens your home page. It greets you by name (Welcome) and lists Your engagements:

  • To do: what is waiting for you, for example Working papers awaiting review or Acceptance awaiting the partner's sign-off, with the engagement it belongs to. Click a line to go there.
  • One card per engagement: the client, the period end and the kind of engagement; the current phase and its Phase deadline; Report due by; the engagement partner; and whether the acceptance is signed (Acceptance signed or Acceptance to sign).
  • A bar and a percentage show how far the file has got. Click Continue to open the engagement.
  • An archived engagement shows Archived on a date and File locked. Click Open to read it; nothing in it can be changed.

If you see No engagements, you are not yet on any team: ask the engagement partner or manager to add you. The + New engagement button appears for everyone except read-only users.

How an audit file is organised

Every file follows the same four phases, in the order of the audit. The engagement's dashboard shows one ring per phase, with the percentage done, and the groups of tasks beside it.

Phase (top bar)On the dashboardTask groups
PlanningPlanning and Risk IdentificationP1 to P7: acceptance and continuance, independence and team, understanding the business, entity-level controls and IT, fraud risks, materiality, planning review and approval
Strategy and risksStrategy and Risk AssessmentS1 to S6: transactions and walkthroughs, controls and IT, combined risk assessment, specific risk areas, audit procedure design, audit strategy memorandum
ExecutionExecutionE1 to E6: tests of controls, control updates, substantive procedures, specific procedures, registers and opening balances
ConclusionConclusion and ReportingC1 to C6: audit differences, final review, representations, review and approval, statutory reports and communications, documentation and archiving

Each task has a code (for example P6.1) that stays the same on every file, so the whole team speaks the same language. Click a group to see its Task list with each task's status and due date. Some papers are Conditional papers: they only come onto the file, and into the percentage, when an answer elsewhere calls for them.

The engagement dashboard also shows My tasks (what is open and assigned to you), Review notes and findings (Notes for me, Notes by me, Deficiencies, Misstatements) and a Calendar of the next deadlines, with the overdue ones counted. If some tasks have nobody to prepare them, a warning says so, with Assign them.

Good to know If the dashboard says Profile not finished: finish it to get your tasks, the engagement profile still needs answers. Click Finish the profile: the profile decides which tasks the file needs.

When the screen is working

When AuditISA needs a moment to fetch or save something, a thin moving bar appears along the very top of the screen. Wait until it disappears before you click again. Quick actions do not show the bar at all.
