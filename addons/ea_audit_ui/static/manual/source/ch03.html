Setting up your firm

This chapter helps you bring your colleagues into AuditISA, give each the right role, and set the firm's sign-off rules before the first engagement starts.

Who: the firm administrator (people, settings, audit log, thresholds); partners (sign-off rules and file index).

Where the firm settings are

Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar. What you see depends on your role:

Users and Settings are also in the Apps grid (nine dots).

Adding a team member to the firm

  1. Open Users.
  2. In the top box, type the person's Email and Name, and choose their Role (see the table below).
  3. Click Invite. A green box appears with the Invitation link.
  4. Click Copy and send the link to the person yourself, by email or a private message. They choose their own password: none is created or sent. The link expires after 6 days.

The person appears in the list as Invited, then Active once they have signed in, with their Last login. If their link expired or was lost, click New link on their row (only possible until they first sign in). To change a role, choose another one in their row. To stop someone's access, click Deactivate; Reactivate undoes it. Nothing they did is deleted.

Good to know A role gives no access to a file: only the engagement's team does. After inviting someone to the firm, the engagement partner or manager still adds them to each engagement team.
Careful Some changes are refused, to protect the firm: you cannot change your own access; the firm must keep at least one active administrator; and a person who is the engagement partner of a file cannot be deactivated or moved below partner until that role is transferred.

Further down the Users screen:

  • Allowed email domains: one per line (for example your firm's own domain). Only addresses in these domains can be invited. Empty means no restriction. Click Save.
  • Administration log: who invited whom, role changes, new links, deactivations. A row marked Technical support shows when AuditISA support entered the firm.

Sign-off policy

The Sign-off policy sets the firm-wide rules. Only a partner or the firm administrator can change it.

FieldMeaning
Minimum Reviewer RoleThe lowest rank that may review any task (default: Senior).
Require Distinct Preparer ApproverWhen ticked (the default), the same person cannot be assigned as both preparer and reviewer of a task.
Allow Sole PractitionerAlways off and cannot be changed.
Allowed Email DomainsThe same list as on the Users screen.
Language of the firmThe language new accounts start in.
Partner sign-off required onExtra tasks on which the partner must sign, on top of those the matrix already asks for.

Three rules are fixed for every firm:

  • No self-review. Whoever prepared a paper cannot also review it; AuditISA refuses. A second pair of eyes is the point of a review (ISA 220).
  • One engagement partner per file. Each engagement has exactly one, who must be a partner or the firm administrator. It cannot be created without one. To change partner, use the transfer form. One named person answers for the opinion.
  • Only the engagement partner, or the partner they delegate to, gives the partner sign-offs on that file.

Sign-off matrix

The Sign-off matrix lists every task in audit order with the rank needed to prepare it (Minimum preparer), to review it (Minimum reviewer) and whether a Partner sign-off is needed. By default, for example, acceptance and independence tasks (P1, P2) are prepared by a manager and reviewed by a partner, and most execution tasks are prepared by staff and reviewed by a senior.

  1. To set a firm rule for one task, fill in Firm's minimum preparer, Firm's minimum reviewer or Firm's partner sign-off on its row. Blank means the firm default applies. Rows with a firm rule stand out in colour.
  2. To change many rows, click Export to Excel, fill in columns I, J and K, then click Import from Excel, choose the file and click Import. If one row is wrong, nothing is saved.
Good to know The reviewer must never rank below the preparer: AuditISA raises the reviewer if needed.

Audit file index

The Audit file index lists every task an audit file can hold, in the order of the audit: Order, code, title, section, Phase, tier (Core, Standard, Extended) and whether it is conditional. It is the same for every firm and cannot be edited here. Use the filters Core and Conditional, or group by Phase or Section.

Audit log

The firm administrator alone reads the Audit log: each line shows the Date, the User, the Engagement, the Task, a Summary, and for an answer the full text Before and After. Nobody can edit or delete it. Filter on Answer changes or Firm settings, or group by user, engagement or task.

Substantive analytics thresholds

The firm administrator sets how far a substantive analytical procedure's result may differ from the expectation before it must be investigated (ISA 520), by combined risk: Lower risk (%), Moderate risk (%), Higher risk (%) (defaults 75, 50 and 25). Lower risk allows the widest gap. Open an engagement's tools, then Substantive analytics, and click Firm thresholds. Click Save. Only procedures saved after the change use the new figures.

Email sending

The Settings screen shows Outgoing mail as Sending on or Sending off. AuditISA sets up the firm's outgoing mail when the firm is put into service; to change it, ask AuditISA. While it is off, AuditISA sends no email (no notices by mail, no password reset). On the same screen you manage the Internal projects people log time on (see Recording your time).
