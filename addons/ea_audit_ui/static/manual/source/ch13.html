Review notes and sign-off

This chapter shows you how to review a working paper, raise and clear review notes, and sign as preparer, reviewer, partner or quality reviewer. It also explains why a signature can be voided.

Who: everyone on the team. Each person can sign only in the roles that the firm's sign-off policy allows.

Reviewing in Review mode

Open the paper and click Review in the header. The tips and full requirements are hidden, so you see only the work. Finished tasks fold to one line; click a line to open it. If tasks were closed with No exceptions noted, a blue banner tells you how many: check that each one has a convincing Source. Under Show only:, use Not done to find gaps and Open notes to follow up your points.

Raising a review note

  1. Under the task concerned, click + Review note on this task. For a point on the conclusion, click + Review note on the conclusion in section 3.
  2. Type the point in Raise a review point.
  3. Click Add the note. The note shows your name and the date.

Any team member who can write may raise a note, and so can the quality reviewer. The open notes are counted in the paper's header and shown as a red dot in the step list.

Replying and clearing

  • Reply: anyone on the team can type in Your reply and click Reply. The author of the note is told. A reply does not close the note: the reviewer decides whether the point is resolved.
  • Clear: closes the note, which then reads Cleared. Only two people may clear a note: its author, or a manager or above who did not prepare the paper.

Notes are never deleted. Cleared notes stay on the paper as part of the audit trail.

Good to know A paper with open review notes cannot be signed by the reviewer. Clear the notes first.

Signing a paper

The sign-off row at the bottom of section 3 shows one button or chip per role:

RoleButtonWho
PreparerSign as preparerThe person who did the work, at or above the minimum role of the sign-off policy.
ReviewerSign as reviewerA senior or above (or the policy's minimum), who is not the preparer.
PartnerSign as partnerA partner, where the task requires partner approval.
Quality reviewerSign as quality reviewerThe engagement quality reviewer appointed on the team, where a quality review applies.
  1. Click Save the paper first.
  2. Check the line Still to complete:. Anything listed there blocks signing.
  3. Click your button. It becomes a chip with the role letter (P preparer, R reviewer, A partner approval, Q quality reviewer), your name and the date.

Under the buttons, a line repeats this task's rule, for example "Preparer: Audit staff minimum · Reviewer: Senior minimum · Partner: required". It also tells you your role on the engagement and the roles you may sign in. The preparer always signs first. On a very simple engagement the preparer signs, then the partner.

Some papers also have a Section conclusion box. Where it appears, a senior or manager clicks Reviewed (senior/manager) before signing the paper as reviewer.

No self-review

If you signed a paper as preparer, AuditISA will not let you review it. Another team member must do that. The firm's sign-off policy can also stop the same person being both preparer and reviewer in the task assignment. A review only means something if a second person looks at the work with fresh eyes (ISA 220).

Why a signature is voided

A signature covers the paper exactly as it stood when it was signed. AuditISA keeps a fingerprint of the answers and the attached files at that moment. If someone changes an answer, attaches a file or removes one afterwards, the fingerprint no longer matches. The review signatures are then voided automatically with the reason "Paper content changed after sign-off". A reviewer cannot be taken to approve work they never saw.

  • Voided signatures are listed under Voided signatures, with who signed, when and why the signature was voided.
  • If no valid reviewer or partner signature is left, the paper goes back to draft and must be signed again.
  • The flag the wording has changed since means the standard text of the paper was updated after you signed. Your signature stays valid, but it is worth a look.
  • The flag signed by non-assignee means someone other than the assigned person signed.

Removing your own sign-off

  1. Click your own chip (its hint reads Your sign-off: click to remove it).
  2. Confirm with Remove my sign-off, or click Cancel.

Only the person who signed can remove that sign-off. If someone signed after you, their sign-off must be removed first. The paper's status follows the signatures that remain. Every removal is recorded in the activity trail.

The quality reviewer

The engagement quality reviewer reads every paper and raises review notes, but does not change the team's work. They record their own review on C4.2 Engagement Quality Review, which only the appointed quality reviewer completes and signs.
