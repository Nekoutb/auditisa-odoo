Signing in for the first time

This chapter takes you from the invitation link you received to your first screen in AuditISA, and shows you how to sign in again, recover a forgotten password and set your language and profile.

Who: everyone in the firm.

Your personal invitation link

Nobody creates a password for you, and nobody sends you one. Instead, your firm administrator adds you to the firm in AuditISA and gives you a personal invitation link, usually by email or by a message. With that link you choose your own password. Nobody else ever knows it, not even the administrator or AuditISA.

  • The link is yours alone: do not forward it.
  • It works once. After you have set your password, it no longer opens anything.
  • It expires after 6 days. If it has expired, or if you lost it, ask your firm administrator for a new one: they click New link next to your name.
Good to know The very first administrator of a firm receives a different link from AuditISA. It works once and expires after 72 hours. The page says Welcome, asks for a password of at least 12 characters (Password (at least 12 characters) and Repeat the password) and ends with Save and log in.

Setting your password

  1. Open the invitation link in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
  2. Check that your email address is shown. It is your login name for AuditISA.
  3. Check or correct your name.
  4. Type the password you have chosen, then type it a second time to confirm it.
  5. Click the button at the bottom of the page. AuditISA signs you in and opens your home page.
Tip Choose a long password that you use nowhere else, for example four unrelated words. Audit files hold your clients' confidential information.

Your home page may be empty at first. Being in the firm does not open any file: an engagement appears once someone on its team adds you and you accept. Your invitations to teams wait under the cog, in My invitations, and the bell tells you when a new one arrives. Open the invitation and click Accept (or Decline).

Signing in later

  1. Go to your firm's AuditISA address. Save it as a bookmark the first time.
  2. Type your email address and your password.
  3. Click Log in.

To leave, click Sign out at the right of the top bar. Do this every time on a shared or public computer.

Careful If you see a band reading STAGING - test data, no email at the bottom of the screen, you are on a test copy, not on your firm's real tool. Nothing you enter there counts.

If you forget your password

The sign-in page offers a link to reset your password only when your firm's email sending is switched on. The tool then emails you a link to choose a new password.

  1. On the sign-in page, click the reset password link under the password box.
  2. Enter your email address and confirm.
  3. Open the email from AuditISA and follow its link to choose a new password.

If you do not see the link, your firm's email sending is off. Ask your firm administrator for help.

Two-step verification

You can protect your account with a second step at sign-in: after your password, AuditISA asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator). Someone who learns your password still cannot get in without your phone. Partners and administrators in particular should switch it on, because they sign off files and manage the firm.

  1. Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar, then Preferences.
  2. Open the Account Security tab.
  3. Click the button to enable two-factor authentication and confirm your password.
  4. Scan the square code shown on screen with your authenticator app.
  5. Type the six-digit code the app shows, then confirm.
Good to know API keys are switched off on every firm's server, for every user: they would let someone bypass two-step verification.

Choosing your language

AuditISA works in English and in French. Click EN or FR in the top bar at any time. The page reloads in that language and AuditISA keeps your choice for your next visits. New accounts start in the firm's language, which the firm administrator sets; each person then chooses their own.

Your profile

The right of the top bar shows your name, your role in the firm and your initials. To see or change your own details:

  1. Click the cog (Settings), then Preferences.
  2. Change what you need, for example your email signature or how you receive notifications.
  3. To change your password, open the Account Security tab and click Change password. You type your current password, then the new one.
  4. Save.
Good to know You cannot change your own role. Only the firm administrator changes roles, and never their own.
