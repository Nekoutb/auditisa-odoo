Your engagement team

Only the people on an engagement's team can open its file. This chapter shows how to invite a colleague, give them a role, answer an invitation, and take someone off the team.

Who: a manager, a partner or the firm administrator who is on the team can invite members and change roles. Only a partner can give or take away the engagement partner role. The engagement quality reviewer cannot change the team.

Seeing who is on the team

  1. Open the engagement.
  2. Click the tools icon (the four squares, Engagement tools) in the top bar.
  3. Click the Engagement team tile.

The list shows each member, their Email, Role and Status (Invited, Accepted or Declined), with the dates Invited on and Answered on.

Inviting a member

  1. In the Engagement team list, click Invite a member. (On the Engagement record, the button is called Invite to the team.)
  2. Type the person's Email. For someone new to the firm, also type their Name.
  3. Choose the Team Role: Staff, Senior, Manager, Senior manager, Director, Partner or Engagement quality reviewer.
  4. Click Invite.

The person sees the invitation in three places: the bell (Notifications), the To do card on their home page (Invitation to answer), and My invitations. No email is sent at this stage.

Good to know Invitations go only to email addresses on the firm's allowed domains. The firm administrator keeps that list. An address that is not yet known to the system gets a new account at staff rank.

Rules on roles

RoleRule
PartnerOne engagement partner per engagement, who must be a partner or the firm administrator. To change who holds it, use Transfer the partner role (see Starting a new engagement).
Engagement quality reviewerMust be a partner or hold the firm role of engagement quality reviewer. They must not be on the team in another role, and must not have been engagement partner of the same client in the two years before this period end (the cooling-off period of ISQM 2). Tick Objectivity declaration to appoint them. Appointing one adds task C4.2 to the file.
OthersAny member of the firm from an allowed domain. A role on the team does not change the person's firm role.

Answering an invitation

  1. Click the gear icon (Settings), then My invitations. You can also click the invitation in the bell.
  2. Open the invitation. It shows the Engagement name, the Client, the Fiscal year and your role.
  3. Click Accept, or click Decline and give a Reason.
Careful By accepting, you confirm that you are independent of this client for this year. If you have any doubt about your independence (a family link, a financial interest, a past role at the client), speak to the engagement partner before you accept.

Until you accept, you cannot open the file. A decline and its reason are written to the engagement's activity trail, so the partner knows why.

Good to know The independence conclusion for the whole engagement is documented on task P2.1. The firm's yearly independence declarations are collected outside AuditISA.

Changing a role

In the Engagement team list, click the member's Role and choose another. The role cell is open only to a manager or above, on a file that is not archived. The engagement partner's role cannot be changed this way: transfer it instead.

Removing someone from the team

  1. Open the Engagement record from the gear icon.
  2. Go to the Team tab.
  3. Click the bin icon at the end of the member's line, then save.

The tasks that person was preparing, reviewing or assigned to go back to unassigned, and the dashboard warns that tasks have no preparer. The removal is written to the activity trail. The engagement partner cannot be removed while they are the only partner: transfer the role first.

Good to know Nobody can change the team of an archived engagement. The file is locked.
