Clients

Every engagement belongs to a client. This chapter shows you how to create a client record, what each field is used for, and how to keep the record up to date from one year to the next.

Who: anyone in the firm can read client records. Staff, seniors and managers can create a new client. Only a partner or the firm administrator can change or archive an existing one.

Where to find your clients

A client record is held once for the whole firm, not per engagement. Everyone in the firm can read it, but each engagement for that client is still visible only to its own team.

  1. Click the gear icon (Settings) in the top bar.
  2. Click Clients. The list shows each client's Name, Legal Form, Sector and Registration Number.
  3. To find a client, type in the search box. The search looks at the name, the registration number and the NIU (the tax identification number).
Tip From inside an engagement, the link About the client → under the engagement title opens that client's record directly.

Creating a client

Create the client before you open its first engagement: the New engagement form asks you to choose an existing client.

  1. In the Clients list, click New.
  2. Type the client's Name as it appears in its articles of association.
  3. Choose the Legal Form (for example SA with a board of directors, SARL, SAS or GIE).
  4. Fill in the other fields you know now (see the table below). Only the name, the legal form and the reporting framework are required.
  5. Click the save icon (the cloud) at the top of the form.

What each field is for

FieldWhat to enter, and why it matters
NameThe legal name. It appears on the dashboard, the reports and the letters.
Legal FormSA (board of directors), SA (administrateur général), SARL, SAS, GIE or Other. Some legal tasks depend on it: for example, one of the OHADA legal procedures does not apply to an SARL or a GIE.
SectorThe client's main activity (trade, services, financial, energy and so on).
Reporting frameworkRevised SYSCOHADA, normal system. It is the only framework offered today and is filled in for you. A new engagement takes it from the client.
Governance ModeHow the entity is run: a board with a combined chairman and managing director, a board with a separate managing director, a sole director, or management by gérant(s).
RegulatorThe sector regulator, if any (for example COBAC for banks or CIMA for insurers).
Registration Number and NIUThe trade register number and the tax identification number. Both can be searched.
AddressThe registered office address.
Share capital (FCFA)Used by the check of equity against half of the share capital in the OHADA legal work. If you leave it empty, the tool looks for the capital in the trial balance.
Mandate Type and Mandate Start YearWhether you were appointed by the statutes (2 financial years) or by the general meeting (6 financial years), and the first year. They let the tool work out when your mandate ends.
Listed, Joint auditor (co-CAC), Public interest entityTick the boxes that apply. They record the client's status.
Good to know Ticking Listed or Public interest entity here does not set how complex the engagement is. That is decided only by the answers to the engagement questionnaire (see Starting a new engagement), so that the level always rests on the facts of the year.

Keeping the record up to date

Review the client record at the start of each year, before you open the new engagement. Changes of share capital, governance or regulator are common and some tasks rely on them.

  1. Open the client from the Clients list.
  2. Change the fields you need. Only a partner or the firm administrator can save a change.
  3. Click the save icon.
Good to know If you change the client's name, the new name is used everywhere at once, including on past engagements.

Retiring a client

A client record is never deleted, because past audit files refer to it. When you no longer act for a client, a partner archives the record instead.

  1. Open the client record.
  2. Click the gear icon Actions at the top of the form, then Archive.

An archived client shows a red Archived ribbon and no longer appears in the list. To see it again, open the search options and choose the Archived filter. Unarchive in the same menu brings it back.
