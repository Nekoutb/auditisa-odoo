The audit file and your tasks

This chapter shows where your work is waiting for you each day: the home page, the engagement dashboard, the task lists, the statuses and deadlines, and the bell.

Your home page

When you sign in, the Dashboard greets you (Welcome) and lists Your engagements. You only see the engagements whose team you belong to.

  • The To do card gathers what needs you across all your files: Working papers awaiting review, Acceptance awaiting the partner's sign-off, Overdue tasks, Significant deficiency to communicate and Invitation to answer. Click a line to go straight there.
  • Each engagement card shows the phase, the Phase deadline, Report due by, the partner, whether the acceptance is signed, and the completion bar. Click Continue → to open it. An archived file reads File locked and opens with Open →.
Tip To jump between files, use the ENGAGEMENTS button in the top bar (Recent engagements, All engagements →). The Search… box finds engagements and tasks, and also searches the methodology guidance (In the methodology) and your team's answers (In the answers).

The engagement dashboard

Inside an engagement, the top bar shows the four phases, Planning, Strategy and risks, Execution and Conclusion, then Tasks. The dashboard shows the OVERALL COMPLETION and one ring per phase.

  1. Click a ring. The groups of that phase open below it, each with tasks done out of tasks in total.
  2. Click a group to see its Task list.
  3. Click a task to open its working paper.

Below the rings you find:

  • a warning such as 3 tasks have no preparer. with Assign them →;
  • REQUIRES YOUR ATTENTION: overdue tasks, each with its delay;
  • My tasks (open and mine), each marked To prepare, In progress, To review or To sign;
  • Review notes and findings: Notes for me, Notes by me, Deficiencies and Misstatements;
  • Calendar: the next deadlines, with a red count of overdue ones.

Which tasks are on your file depends on the engagement questionnaire (see Starting a new engagement). Under a group's task list, Conditional papers lists tasks that are Not counted: they come onto the file when their trigger answers yes.

The "Statutory reports" heading

In the Conclusion phase, the reports the law asks of the statutory auditor sit together under Statutory reports: the opinion and auditor's report (C5.1), the special report on regulated agreements (C5.3), the Article 715 report (C5.4) and the Article 525 certification (C5.10). Each stays a separate task with its own sign-offs. The other letters and legal procedures are under Communications and legal procedures.

The Tasks screen

Click Tasks in the top bar to see every task of the file in one table. Use the filters Mine, All and Open; each shows its count.

The PREPARER, REVIEWER and PARTNER columns carry a small letter (P, R, A). It is grey until that person has signed.

StatusMeaning
NOT STARTEDNobody has worked on the paper yet.
IN PROGRESSThe preparer has started.
IN REVIEWThe preparer has signed; the reviewer's turn.
TO SIGNThe reviewer has signed; waiting for the partner's sign-off.
COMPLETEEvery required signature is given.
NOT APPLICABLEMarked not applicable, with a reason.

Deadlines and late flags

Each task has one due date. A senior or above can set it in the Assignment panel of the paper (Deadline). Without one, the deadline of its phase applies:

PhaseDeadline
Acceptance90 days before the period end
Planning45 days before the period end
Execution90 days after the period end
Conclusion150 days after the period end
ReportDue 6 months after the period end

An open task past its date shows Overdue by and the number of days, in red. Late flags stop once a file is archived.

Notifications

The bell in the top bar counts what is new for you: a paper To review or To sign after a colleague's signature, a Task assigned to you, or an Invitation to a team. Click a line to open the task. See all notifications opens the full list.

Bringing an older file up to the current scope

Who: a partner on the team (not the engagement quality reviewer).

Files opened before 17 November 2026 keep the tasks they were opened with: no task appears or disappears on a live file by itself. To move such a file to the current scope:

  1. Open the Engagement record from the gear icon.
  2. Click Apply the current task scope and confirm.

Tasks the current scope adds come onto the file. Tasks that hold no work and no longer apply are hidden. Tasks that hold work stay. The result is written to the activity trail. The button disappears once applied.
