Completing the audit

This chapter takes you through the Conclusion phase: the final reviews, the representation letter, the reports, the quality review, the completion checklist and the partner's sign-off that lets the report be issued.

Who: the whole team. The quality reviewer completes C4.2. Only a partner signs the checklist and issues the report.

Which tasks you see depends on the client's legal form and on the engagement profile. For example, C5.7 does not apply to an SARL. Every paper works as described in Working on a working paper.

Subsequent events and going concern (C2.2)

C2.2 brings your going concern work to its final conclusion. It starts from the preliminary view in S4.2 and the execution work in E6.3. Carry your procedures up to the report date, make a final inquiry of management, read the minutes since your last reading, and decide whether each event requires an adjustment or a disclosure. Then conclude whether the going concern basis is appropriate and whether a material uncertainty exists. The card at the top repeats the equity figures from the trial balance.

The representation letter (C3.1)

  1. Open Templates in the Tools box to start from the firm's letter.
  2. Add the representations specific to this engagement and the materiality used in the letter.
  3. Have the partner review the draft before it goes to the client.
  4. Attach the signed letter. It must be dated no later than the auditor's report.

C3.1 is always in full documentation, whatever the profile.

Reports and communications

C5.1 Opinion and report records the opinion, the components of the report, going concern, key audit matters, any emphasis or other matter, and the report date. Its tools are Summary of audit differences, Materiality and Findings. The other statutory reports (C5.3, C5.4, C5.10) cover the OHADA special reports. C5.11 records what you told those charged with governance and management, including the control deficiencies from the Findings tool.

Quality review (C4.2)

Where an engagement quality review is required, the quality reviewer evaluates the team's significant judgements. They raise review notes on the papers and record their review on C4.2. Only the appointed quality reviewer completes and signs C4.2. The review must be complete before the report is dated.

Partner review and approval (C4.1, C4.3)

On C4.3 Points Outstanding, list what is still open and clear each point with evidence. On C4.1 Review and Approval, the engagement partner confirms that every planned procedure was performed and every paper is signed. Every point outstanding must be cleared and no review note left open. The misstatements must be evaluated, the quality review complete, and the evidence must support the opinion. Its Tools box links to Review notes, Audit programs and Team.

The completion checklist and issuing the report

Open C6.1 and click Completion checklist. The page has two parts:

  • 1 · Checked by the tool: checks that AuditISA runs on the file by itself: every task addressed, every paper signed and reviewed, every review note cleared, controls and substantive procedures concluded, and significant deficiencies communicated. Each check is green or red, and a red one names the tasks concerned.
  • 2 · Confirmed by the team — a note is required for each answer: thirteen questions, for example "Subsequent events reviewed up to the report date" and "Independence re-confirmed by every team member". Answer Yes or No and write a note. Only a Yes with its note turns green.
  1. When every line is green, the engagement partner clicks Partner sign-off: ready to issue.
  2. The partner then clicks Issue the report and confirms. The report date is recorded.
Careful If an answer changes after the partner has signed, the sign-off is voided. The partner signs again before the report is issued.

Work after the report date

After the report date, only assembly work is allowed. A new finding, misstatement or procedure conclusion is refused unless a manager or above clicks Open, with this reason under Work after the report date and gives the reason. Each item recorded is then named in the activity trail. Close the work after the report date ends it. This keeps the file honest about what was known when the report was signed (ISA 230).
