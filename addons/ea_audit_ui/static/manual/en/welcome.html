Welcome to AuditISA

What AuditISA does, how every engagement file is organised, and what to do while the screen is working.

Who: everyone in the firm.

What AuditISA does

AuditISA holds your firm's engagement files. Each engagement (one client, one financial year) has its own engagement file, built on the International Standards on Auditing (ISA) and on the OHADA rules. The engagement file lists every task of the audit, from accepting the client to archiving the engagement file. You prepare each task, a more senior colleague reviews it, and the partner signs, depending on your firm's sign-off policy. Only the people on an engagement's team can open that engagement: your role in the firm alone does not give you access to an engagement.

How an engagement file is organised

Every engagement file follows the same four phases, in the order of the audit. The engagement's dashboard shows one ring per phase, with the percentage done, and the groups of tasks beside it.

Phase (top bar)On the dashboardTask groups
PlanningPlanning and Risk IdentificationP1 to P7: acceptance and continuance, independence and team, understanding the business, entity-level controls and IT, fraud risks, materiality, planning review and approval
Strategy and risksStrategy and Risk AssessmentS1 to S6: transactions and walkthroughs, controls and IT, combined risk assessment, specific risk areas, audit procedure design, audit strategy memorandum
ExecutionExecutionE1 to E6: tests of controls, control updates, substantive procedures, specific procedures, registers and opening balances
ConclusionConclusion and ReportingC1 to C6: audit differences, final review, representations, review and approval, statutory reports and communications, documentation and archiving

Each task has a code (for example P6.1) that stays the same on every engagement file, so the whole team speaks the same language. Click a group to see its Task list with each task's status and due date. Some working papers are Conditional papers: they only come onto the engagement file, and into the percentage, when an answer elsewhere calls for them.

The engagement dashboard also shows My tasks (what is open and assigned to you), Review notes and findings (Notes for me, Notes by me, Deficiencies, Misstatements) and a Calendar of the next deadlines, with the overdue ones counted. If some tasks have nobody to prepare them, a warning says so, with Assign them.

Good to know If the dashboard says Profile not finished: finish it to get your tasks, the engagement profile still needs answers. Click Finish the profile: the profile decides which tasks the engagement file needs.

When the screen is working

When AuditISA needs a moment to fetch or save something, a thin moving bar appears along the very top of the screen. Wait until it disappears before you click again. Quick actions do not show the bar at all.
