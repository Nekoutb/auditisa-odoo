Review notes

Review a working paper, then raise, answer and clear review notes on it.

Who: any team member who can write may raise a note, and so can the quality reviewer. Anyone on the team can reply.

Reviewing in Review mode

Open the working paper and click Review in the header. The tips and full requirements are hidden, so you see only the work. Finished tasks fold to one line; click a line to open it. If tasks were closed with No exceptions noted, a blue banner tells you how many: check that each one has a convincing Source. Under Show only:, use Not done to find gaps and Open notes to follow up your points.

Raising a review note

  1. Under the task concerned, click + Review note on this task. For a point on the conclusion, click + Review note on the conclusion in section 3.
  2. Type the point in Raise a review point.
  3. Click Add the note. The note shows your name and the date.

The open notes are counted in the working paper's header and shown as a red dot in the step list.

Replying and clearing

  • Reply: anyone on the team can type in Your reply and click Reply. The author of the note is told. A reply does not close the note: the reviewer decides whether the point is resolved.
  • Clear: closes the note, which then reads Cleared. Only these people may clear a note: its author, or a manager or above who did not prepare the working paper.

Notes are never deleted. Cleared notes stay on the working paper as part of the audit trail.

Good to know A working paper with open review notes cannot be signed by the reviewer. Clear the notes first.
