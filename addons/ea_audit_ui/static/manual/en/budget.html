Budget and actual

Compare the hours charged on an engagement with its budget, per person and per phase.

Who: the partner or manager of the team sees every member and types the budget; other team members see their own row only.

Reading the budget

Inside an engagement, click the cog and choose Budget and actual. Each cell reads actual hours / budget hours, per person and per phase (Planning, Strategy and risks, Execution, Conclusion). Figures over budget show in red.

  • The partner or manager of the team sees every member and types the budget hours in the boxes.
  • Other team members see their own row only.
Good to know Hours charged to a task count in that task's phase. Hours without a task count in the phase the engagement was in on that day. Choosing the task keeps the budget follow-up accurate.
