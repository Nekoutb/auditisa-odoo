The risk register

Record every risk of the engagement, link it to the accounts it affects and to the work that answers it.

Who: the engagement team records the risks; a partner approves a rebuttal of the presumed revenue-fraud risk and any risk added after planning, depending on your firm's sign-off policy.

The risk register (S3.1)

The Risk register (S3.1) lists every risk of the engagement: its category (Business, Fraud or Error), its level (Financial statements or Assertion), its inherent rating, whether it is significant, the lead indexes it touches and its status (Identified, Response planned, Response executed, Concluded).

  1. Click Add a risk, describe it and rate its likelihood and magnitude.
  2. Open the risk and click Link to a lead index to say which accounts and assertions it affects. The link also appears in the Risks column of Significant accounts.
  3. Click Link a program step to show how the risk is answered.

The presumed risks

Two presumed risks are on every engagement: Revenue fraud and Management override. Management override cannot be rebutted. To rebut the revenue-fraud risk, a team member clicks Propose a rebuttal and writes why; a partner then clicks Approve the rebuttal or Reject the rebuttal. The person who proposed cannot approve their own proposal, so a second person always checks this judgement (ISA 240).

Good to know A risk added once planning is closed needs a partner to click Approve the addition.
