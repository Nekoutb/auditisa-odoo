Sign-off policy

The firm-wide rules on who may review and sign, valid for every engagement.

Who: partners and the firm administrator. Only they can change it.

The fields of the policy

Click the cog (Settings), then Sign-off policy.

FieldMeaning
Minimum Reviewer RoleThe lowest rank that may review any task (default: Senior).
Require Distinct Preparer ApproverWhen ticked (the default), the same person cannot be assigned as both preparer and reviewer of a task.
Allow Sole PractitionerAlways off and cannot be changed.
Allowed Email DomainsThe same list as on the Users screen.
Language of the firmThe language new accounts start in.
Partner sign-off required onExtra tasks on which the partner must sign, on top of those the matrix already asks for.

Rules fixed for every firm

  • No self-review: whoever prepared a working paper cannot also review it; AuditISA refuses.
  • One engagement partner per engagement: each engagement has exactly one, who must be a partner or the firm administrator. It cannot be created without one. To change partner, use the transfer form.
  • Only the engagement partner, or the partner they delegate to, gives the partner sign-offs on that engagement.
