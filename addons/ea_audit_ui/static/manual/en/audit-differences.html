The summary of audit differences

The summary of audit differences gathers every adjustment of the engagement, to be reviewed, posted and sent to the client.

Who: a reviewer posts the adjustments, depending on your firm's sign-off policy. The quality reviewer reads but does not change them.

The summary of audit differences

Open it from Summary of audit differences in the Tools box of C1.1. The differences come from the working papers. To add or change one, open its working paper. Click a line to see where it came from. The tabs are 1 Uncorrected, 2 Corrected, 3 Disclosures, 4 Cash flow, 5 Other factors and 6 Tax and conclusion.

  • Brackets mean that the line reduces profit or the balance.
  • Differences below the clearly trivial level are kept aside under Clearly trivial. They are not added to the total, and they are not lost (ISA 450).
  • The Total uncorrected (before tax) and the materiality bar count every adjustment, posted or not.
  • In 6 Tax and conclusion, enter the Tax rate (%) and write the Team conclusion: are the uncorrected differences material, alone or together?

Reviewing and posting a line

  1. Use the Adjustments to review filter to see the lines not posted yet.
  2. If something is wrong, click Review note, write what the preparer should look at, and click Send. The line cannot be posted until the note is cleared with Clear the note.
  3. When you agree with the line, click Post.

The person who prepared an adjustment cannot post it. Nor can the quality reviewer. Your firm's sign-off policy for C1.1 decides who may post. To change a posted line, click Unpost first. This is logged, and the line shows changed since posted until it is posted again. A posted line can still be marked as corrected by management.

The client copy

Only posted lines make up the client's list. Click Download the client copy (posted only) to get the Excel file that you send to management with your request to correct. The Posted (client copy) column shows which lines it holds.

The C1.1 working paper

C1.1 Summary of Audit Differences is where you conclude. A card at the top shows the totals from the summary. Its tasks follow ISA 450: accumulate, classify, communicate, obtain the reasons for not correcting, reassess materiality and evaluate individually and in aggregate. C1.1 is never lightened, whatever the profile.
