The top bar

The bar at the top of the screen stays in place on every page and takes you anywhere in AuditISA.

Who: everyone in the firm.

What each item does

From left to right, the top bar holds:

ItemWhat it does
The A logo and your firm's nameTakes you back to your home page (Dashboard) from anywhere.
Dashboard and EngagementsShown when you are not inside an engagement. Engagements lists all the engagements you are a team member of.
Planning, Strategy and risks, Execution, Conclusion, TasksShown when you are inside an engagement. Each phase name opens that part of the engagement's dashboard. Tasks lists every task of the engagement file.
ENGAGEMENTS switcherShows the engagement you are in (or Select engagement). Click it to see your Recent engagements and jump to another one, or click All engagements.
Search…Type at least two letters. Results come in four groups: Engagements, Tasks, In the methodology (the guidance text) and In the answers (what the team has written).
Engagement tools (four squares)Shown inside an engagement. Opens the engagement's tools, such as the trial balance, the analytics and the lists of findings.
Timesheet (clock)Shown outside an engagement. Opens your timesheet (see Recording your time).
Apps (nine dots)Lists the parts of AuditISA you may open: Audit (engagements and engagement files), Timesheet, and, for the firm administrator, Users and Settings.
Help (?)Opens this user manual at the page about the screen you are on (Help on this page). Search the manual or browse its contents from there.
Notifications (bell)A red figure shows how many messages you have not read. Click a message to go straight to the task, engagement or invitation it is about. See all notifications opens the full list.
Settings (cog)A short menu. Inside an engagement it starts with Engagement record, Log time on this engagement and Budget and actual. It always holds Clients, My invitations and Preferences. Partners also see the firm's sign-off rules and file index; the firm administrator also sees the audit log, Users and Settings.
EN / FRSwitches the whole tool between English and French. The page reloads in the language you chose, and AuditISA remembers it next time.
Your name, role and initialsShows who is signed in and your role in the firm (for example Manager or Quality reviewer).
Sign outEnds your session. Always sign out on a shared computer.

On a phone, the bar takes several lines: your firm, Help, the bell, the cog and Sign out first, then the switcher, the search and the menu. EN / FR and your name are left out to save room: change the language in Preferences.

Tip Press Esc or click anywhere else to close an open panel (switcher, bell, cog, apps or search results).
