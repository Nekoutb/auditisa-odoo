Archiving the engagement file

Assemble and archive the engagement file once the report is issued, and add to it later by addendum only.

Who: any accepted team member who can write may archive (not the quality reviewer). Legal holds need a manager or above.

The assembly period

After the report date you have sixty days to assemble the final engagement file (ISA 230). Open C6.2 and click Assemble and archive. The page shows how many days are left. The counter turns amber when fewer than fifteen days remain. During this time, do only assembly work: tidy the working papers, file the signed documents and clear what is left. See Completing the audit for work after the report date.

Archiving the engagement file

  1. On the Assemble and archive page, check the column on the left. Every line must be green.
  2. Under Before archiving, answer the two confirmations with Yes and a note: the signed report, financial statements and representation letter are filed, and the permanent file is updated.
  3. Read What archiving does.
  4. Type the client's name in the box to confirm, then click Archive the file.
  5. If the sixty days have passed, AuditISA asks for the reason for the late archive. The reason is kept in the trail.
Careful Archiving cannot be undone. An archived engagement file can be opened again for reading only.

What archiving does

  • The whole engagement file becomes read-only: nothing can be edited or deleted. Each page shows Read only: this engagement is archived.
  • A sealed copy is made. It holds every working paper (as PDF where possible, otherwise as a web page), all the evidence, and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of each file. The fingerprint proves later that nothing has changed.
  • The sealed copy is kept on the server and in the backups. A team member can click Download the sealed copy. Each download is logged.
  • The team and inspectors can still open and read the engagement file. Every opening is listed in the Access log.

Adding something after archiving: addenda

Sometimes something must be added after the engagement file is closed, such as a late document or an answer to an inspector. You do not change the archive: you add an addendum (ISA 230 ¶16).

  1. On the archive page, under Addenda after archiving (ISA 230 ¶16), click Add an addendum.
  2. Write why: what is added, and who asked for it. Attach the document if there is one.
  3. Click Add the addendum.

An addendum adds to the engagement file. It never changes or removes what was archived. The list shows who added it, when and why.

Retention and legal hold

An archived engagement file is kept for at least ten years from the report date, as OHADA practice requires. It cannot be deleted before that date. If a dispute or an inquiry is expected, a manager or above can click Place a legal hold and give a reason. While the hold is on, the engagement file cannot be deleted, even after its retention period. Release the legal hold lifts it.
