Your engagement team

Only the people on an engagement's team can open it: see who they are, invite a colleague, change a role or take someone off the team.

Who: a manager, a partner or the firm administrator who is on the team can invite members and change roles. Only a partner can give or take away the engagement partner role. The engagement quality reviewer cannot change the team.

Seeing who is on the team

  1. Open the engagement.
  2. Click the tools icon (the four squares, Engagement tools) in the top bar.
  3. Click the Engagement team tile.

The list shows each member, their Email, Role and Status (Invited, Accepted or Declined), with the dates Invited on and Answered on.

Inviting a member

  1. In the Engagement team list, click Invite a member. (On the Engagement record, the button is called Invite to the team.)
  2. Type the person's Email. Only people already in the firm can be invited (see below).
  3. Choose the Team Role: Staff, Senior, Manager, Senior manager, Director, Partner or Engagement quality reviewer.
  4. Click Invite.

The person sees the invitation in three places: the bell (Notifications), a large card at the top of their home page (You are invited to … as …, with Accept and Decline), and My invitations. No email is sent at this stage.

The window reminds you: Only people already in the firm can be invited. Someone new is added first by the firm administrator, on the Users screen. If you type the address of someone who is not in the firm yet, a message titled Not possible yet says This person is not in the firm yet. Ask your firm administrator to add them first (Users screen); they can add them to this engagement at the same time. The firm administrator does both in one step, with Also add to engagement (see Users of the firm).

Good to know Invitations go only to email addresses on the firm's allowed domains. The firm administrator keeps that list. Nobody gets a new account from this window: only the firm administrator adds people to the firm.

Rules on roles

RoleRule
PartnerOne engagement partner per engagement, who must be a partner or the firm administrator. To change who holds it, use Transfer the partner role (see Acceptance and the engagement partner).
Engagement quality reviewerMust be a partner or hold the firm role of engagement quality reviewer. They must not be on the team in another role, and must not have been engagement partner of the same client in the two years before this period end (the cooling-off period of ISQM 2). Tick Objectivity declaration to appoint them. Appointing one adds task C4.2 to the engagement file.
OthersAny member of the firm from an allowed domain. A role on the team does not change the person's firm role.

Changing a role

In the Engagement team list, click the member's Role and choose another. The role cell is open only to a manager or above, on an engagement that is not archived. The engagement partner's role cannot be changed this way: transfer it instead.

Removing someone from the team

  1. Open the Engagement record from the gear icon.
  2. Go to the Team tab.
  3. Click the bin icon at the end of the member's line, then save.

The tasks that person was preparing, reviewing or assigned to go back to unassigned, and the dashboard warns that tasks have no preparer. The removal is written to the activity trail. The engagement partner cannot be removed while they are the only partner: transfer the role first.

Good to know Nobody can change the team of an archived engagement. The engagement file is locked.
