Journal entry testing

Select the journal entries that show signs of possible management override, test each one on the same screen, and conclude on journal entry testing (E3.1).

Who: the engagement team. Roles that only read (for example the quality reviewer) see the page in read-only mode.

One screen

Open Engagement dashboard › Tools › Journal entry testing. Everything is on one screen, without tabs. This is the work ISA 240 asks for on journal entries.

Four boxes at the top give the summary:

BoxWhat it shows
General ledger in useIts lines, its entries and when it was loaded.
Entries selectedThe entries found by at least one criterion.
TestedHow many of the selection are tested, without exceptions and with exceptions.
Findings raisedThe findings, and the adjustments sent to the summary of audit differences.

Criteria

On the left, Criteria lists each criterion with a tick box and the number of entries it finds, in four groups:

GroupCriteria
When it was postedPosted on a Sunday, Posted on a Saturday, Posted on a public holiday, Last 5 days of the year or first 5 days after (5 by default).
Who posted itSame person prepared and approved, No approver recorded, Posted by a person who rarely posts entries.
What it saysRound amounts, Just below an approval limit, Missing or vague description, Rarely used or suspense accounts.
Unusual account pairingsRevenue against cash, Expenses paid in cash above a limit, Fixed assets against revenue, Payables against receivables, Revenue debited after year end, Equity against income or expense.

The selection is every entry found by at least one criterion. Tick criteria to show only the entries they find; with nothing ticked, the table shows the whole selection. Clear the criteria removes the ticks.

Under the criteria, one line gives the settings, set once for the engagement: the round amount (1 000 000), the approval limit (5 000 000, with a band of 10 %), the cash expense limit (500 000), the days around the year end (5), the rare poster (below 2 % of the entries), the rare account (fewer than 3 entries), the public holidays of the year (Cameroon calendar) and the vague words. Click Change the settings to adjust them.

The selected entries

On the right, the table shows each selected entry: Entry, Date (with the weekday), Journal, Accounts (debit → credit), Amount, Prepared / approved, Why selected and Test (To test, No exception or Exception). Click Export the selection for the client (Excel) to send the list to the client.

  1. Click an entry: its test opens below it.
  2. Answer the four questions, each Yes, No or Not applicable: Supporting document obtained and agreed to the entry (amount, date, accounts); Entry approved by an appropriate person, other than its preparer; Business reason understood and consistent with our knowledge of the entity; Recorded in the right accounts, period and amount.
  3. Write what you did in Work done and result. Cite the attachments as [A1], [A2].
  4. If something is wrong, click Raise a finding (the finding goes to the Findings tool, linked to the entry and to E3.1) or Add an adjustment (it goes to the summary of audit differences and waits for the reviewer).

The entry's conclusion follows the answers: Exception as soon as one answer is No; No exception when all four are answered and none is No.

Findings, adjustments and sign-off

At the bottom, Findings from journal entry testing lists the findings raised (Finding, Entry, Kind, Stage), and Adjustments to the summary of audit differences lists the adjustments (Entry, Heading, Adjustment proposed, Amount, Status).

Under Conclusion and sign-off, click Sign as preparer; the reviewer then clicks Sign as reviewer. This signs working paper E3.1, with the usual sign-off rules.

Tip Entries reviewed before this screen keep their review: Cleared reads as No exception, Problem as Exception, and the note as Work done and result.
