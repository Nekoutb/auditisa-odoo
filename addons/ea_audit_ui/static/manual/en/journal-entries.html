Who: the engagement team. Roles that only read (for example the quality reviewer) see the page in read-only mode.

Journal entries (E3.1)

The Journal entries page has four tabs:

  1. 1 Ledger: the ledger tested and its checks. If no ledger is ready, click The ledgers.
  2. 2 Criteria: turn each criterion on or off; the entries it catches are counted at once. Thresholds and own rules sets amounts and your own rules. Click Run the criteria.
  3. 3 Selected entries: open each entry, look at its documents, then clear it or click Raise a finding (Misstatement, Matter for attention or Control deficiency).
  4. 4 Conclusion: the counts cleared and with a problem. Click Record as the S5.4 design to keep the selection as the design, and Export the E3.1 workbook for the engagement file.
