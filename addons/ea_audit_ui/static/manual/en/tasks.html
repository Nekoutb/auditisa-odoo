The Tasks screen and deadlines

The Tasks screen lists every task of the engagement, with who prepares, reviews and signs it, its due date and its status.

Who: every member of the engagement team. A senior or above can set a task's deadline.

The Tasks screen

Click Tasks in the top bar to see every task of the engagement in one table. Use the filters Mine, All and Open; each shows its count.

The PREPARER, REVIEWER and PARTNER columns carry a small letter (P, R, A). It is grey until that person has signed.

StatusMeaning
NOT STARTEDNobody has worked on the working paper yet.
IN PROGRESSThe preparer has started.
IN REVIEWThe preparer has signed; the reviewer's turn.
TO SIGNThe reviewer has signed; waiting for the partner's sign-off.
COMPLETEEvery required signature is given.
NOT APPLICABLEMarked not applicable, with a reason.

Deadlines and late flags

Each task has one due date. A senior or above can set it in the Assignment panel of the working paper (Deadline). Without one, the deadline of its phase applies:

PhaseDeadline
Acceptance90 days before the period end
Planning45 days before the period end
Execution90 days after the period end
Conclusion150 days after the period end
ReportDue 6 months after the period end

An open task past its date shows Overdue by and the number of days, in red. Late flags stop once an engagement is archived.
