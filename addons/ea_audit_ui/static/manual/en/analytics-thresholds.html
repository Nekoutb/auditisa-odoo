Substantive analytics thresholds

How far a substantive analytical procedure's result may differ from the expectation before it must be investigated.

Who: the firm administrator only.

Setting the firm thresholds

The thresholds are set by combined risk, as a percentage of performance materiality (ISA 520): Lower risk (%), Moderate risk (%), Higher risk (%) (defaults 75, 50 and 25). Lower risk allows the widest gap.

  1. Open an engagement's tools, then Substantive analytics.
  2. Click Firm thresholds.
  3. Change the three figures and click Save.
Good to know Only procedures saved after the change use the new figures.
