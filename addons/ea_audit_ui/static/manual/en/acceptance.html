Acceptance and the engagement partner

The first phase of a new engagement: decide to take on or keep the client, then move the engagement to planning.

Who: the engagement team prepares the acceptance tasks; the engagement partner signs P1.1 and moves the engagement to planning. Only the engagement partner or the firm administrator can transfer the partner role.

Acceptance and continuance

The new engagement starts in the Acceptance phase. Its first group of tasks, Acceptance and continuance (P1), covers the decision to take on or keep the client: the acceptance or continuance checklist (P1.1), the predecessor auditor (P1.2, first year only), the preconditions for an audit (P1.3), the engagement letter (P1.4) and the decision on an engagement quality review (P1.5). The independence conclusion sits in P2.1.

The engagement can move to planning only when:

  • P1.1 is complete and concluded "accept";
  • every required acceptance check is answered "yes";
  • the engagement partner has signed P1.1;
  • exactly one engagement partner has accepted;
  • the review notes on P1 are cleared.

Until then, the home page shows Acceptance to sign on the engagement card. Then the engagement partner clicks Advance to planning on the Engagement record.

The engagement partner

Each engagement has exactly one engagement partner, who must be a partner or the firm administrator. The partner gives the partner sign-offs on the key working papers and has the last word on the engagement.

  • To hand the role to another partner, open the Engagement record (gear icon) and click Transfer the partner role. Choose the New engagement partner and the Outgoing partner's new role, then Transfer. Only the engagement partner or the firm administrator can do this.
  • The engagement partner may name a Partner delegate on the same record: another partner on the team who can give the partner sign-offs in their place.
