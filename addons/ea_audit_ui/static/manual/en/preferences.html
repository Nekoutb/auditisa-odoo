Your preferences and sign-in security

Your own details, your password, your language and two-step verification at sign-in.

Who: everyone in the firm, each for their own account.

Two-step verification

You can protect your account with a second step at sign-in: after your password, AuditISA asks for a six-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone (for example Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator). Someone who learns your password still cannot get in without your phone. Partners and administrators in particular should switch it on.

  1. Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar, then Preferences.
  2. Open the Security tab.
  3. Click the button to enable two-factor authentication and confirm your password.
  4. Scan the square code shown on screen with your authenticator app.
  5. Type the six-digit code the app shows, then confirm.
Good to know API keys are switched off on every firm's server, for every user: they would let someone bypass two-step verification.

Choosing your language

AuditISA works in English and in French. Click EN or FR in the top bar at any time. The page reloads in that language and AuditISA keeps your choice for your next visits. New accounts start in the firm's language, which the firm administrator sets; each person then chooses their own.

Your profile

The right of the top bar shows your name, your role in the firm and your initials. To see or change your own details:

  1. Click the cog (Settings), then Preferences.
  2. Change what you need, for example your email signature or how you receive notifications.
  3. To change your password, open the Security tab and click Change password. You type your current password, then the new one.
  4. Click Update Preferences.
Good to know You cannot change your own role. Only the firm administrator changes roles, and never their own.
