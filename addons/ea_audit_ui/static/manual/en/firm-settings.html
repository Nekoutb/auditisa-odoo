Firm settings and email

The Settings screen shows whether the firm sends email and holds the internal projects people log time on.

Who: the firm administrator only.

Where to find it

Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar, then Settings. It is also in the Apps grid (nine dots).

Email sending

The Settings screen shows Outgoing mail as Sending on or Sending off. AuditISA sets up the firm's outgoing mail when the firm is put into service; to change it, ask AuditISA. While it is off, AuditISA sends no email (no notices by mail, no password reset). On the same screen you manage the Internal projects people log time on (see Recording your time).
