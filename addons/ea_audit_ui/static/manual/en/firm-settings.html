Firm settings and email

The Settings screen shows whether the firm sends email and holds the internal projects people log time on.

Who: the firm administrator only.

Where to find it

Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar, then Settings. It is also in the Apps grid (nine dots).

Email sending

The Settings screen shows Outgoing mail as Sending on or Sending off (on a test copy: Off on the test environment). The firm's email is set up by AuditISA when the firm is created: the firm gives AuditISA the settings of its own mailbox, the firm's server tests them without sending any message, and switches sending on only if the test passes. Nobody changes them from this screen: AuditISA sets up the firm's outgoing mail when the firm is put into service. To change it, ask AuditISA.

  • While sending is on, the sign-in page offers Forgot your password?, and the Users screen offers Send by email next to Copy for invitation links.
  • While it is off, AuditISA sends no email (no notices by mail, no password reset): copy the invitation and new password links and send them yourself.

On the same screen you manage the Internal projects people log time on (see Recording your time).
