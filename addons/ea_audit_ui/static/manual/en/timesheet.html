Recording your time

Enter your hours on engagements and internal projects, and submit your week before the deadline.

Who: everyone enters their own hours. Partners and the firm administrator can reopen a locked week.

Opening your timesheet

There are three ways in:

  • Click the clock in the top bar (shown when you are not inside an engagement).
  • Open the Apps grid (nine dots) and click Timesheet.
  • Inside an engagement, click the cog and choose Log time on this engagement. That engagement is then already chosen for you.

The Timesheet page shows one week, Monday to Sunday. Use the arrows to go to the Previous week or the Next week, and This week to come back. Back to the audit returns you to your engagements.

Where you can charge time

The Engagement or project list has two parts:

  • My engagements: only the engagements whose team you belong to and have accepted. You cannot charge time to an engagement you have not been invited to, and an archived engagement takes no more hours.
  • Internal projects: the firm's own work, such as Training, Leave and Administration. The firm administrator adds or archives these under Settings.
Good to know Everyone enters their own hours only. Nobody can enter time for someone else.

Entering hours

  1. Choose the Date (today or an earlier day of the week shown; future days are not offered).
  2. Choose the Engagement or project.
  3. If you wish, choose the Task (optional) you worked on. Leave it at None otherwise.
  4. Type the Hours, for example 2.5.
  5. Add a short Note if it helps your manager understand the time.
  6. Click Charge. The message Hours charged. confirms it, and the hours appear in the week's grid.

You can also correct hours straight in the grid: click a day's box on a row, change the figure and click elsewhere. The grid shows a total per row, per day and for the week.

Careful Hours must be between 0 and 24, and a day cannot add up to more than 24 hours.

Submitting your week

When the week is complete, click Submit the week. The status changes to Submitted and the page shows when you submitted it. If you notice a mistake, click Take back, correct it and submit again. You can do this until the deadline.

The deadline for a week is the Tuesday after it, at 23:59, Douala time. The page always shows it (Deadline: …, Douala time), and a yellow line reminds you when last week still waits to be submitted.

Weeks you missed are listed under Overdue, not submitted. Click one to open it.

Tip Enter your hours each evening and submit every Friday. Time reports and budgets depend on complete weeks.

Reopening a locked week

Who: the firm administrator, or a partner of one of the person's engagements.

Ask a partner or your firm administrator; you cannot reopen your own week. They open Reports on the Timesheet page and, under Reopen a locked week:

  1. Choose the Person.
  2. Type A date of the week (DD/MM/YYYY).
  3. Give a Written reason (at least 10 characters).
  4. Click Reopen for 48 hours.

Each reopening is kept in a list with the reason, who reopened it and until when. The person then has 48 hours to complete and submit the week.
