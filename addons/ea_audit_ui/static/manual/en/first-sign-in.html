Signing in for the first time

From your invitation link to your first screen, then signing in again and recovering a forgotten password.

Who: everyone in the firm.

Your personal invitation link

Nobody creates a password for you, and nobody sends you one. Instead, your firm administrator adds you to the firm in AuditISA and gives you a personal invitation link, usually by email or by a message. With that link you choose your own password. Nobody else ever knows it, not even the administrator or AuditISA.

  • The link is yours alone: do not forward it.
  • It works once. After you have set your password, it no longer opens anything.
  • It expires after 72 hours. If it has expired, or if you lost it, ask your firm administrator for a new one: they click New link next to your name.
Good to know The very first administrator of a firm receives a different link from AuditISA. It works once and expires after 72 hours. The page says Welcome, asks for a password of at least 12 characters (Password (at least 12 characters) and Repeat the password) and ends with Save and log in.

Setting your password

  1. Open the invitation link in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). The page says Welcome to and the name of your firm, then Choose your password to finish setting up your account.
  2. Check that your email address is shown. It is your login name for AuditISA, and it cannot be changed on this page.
  3. Check or correct your name.
  4. Type the password you have chosen, then type it a second time to confirm it.
  5. Click Set my password. AuditISA signs you in and opens your home page.
Tip Choose a long password that you use nowhere else, for example four unrelated words. Engagement files hold your clients' confidential information.

Your home page may be empty at first: it says You are in the firm. Your engagements appear here once a manager adds you to a team and you accept. Being in the firm does not open any engagement. When someone invites you to a team, a large card at the top of your home page says You are invited to … as …: click Accept (or Decline). Your invitations also wait under the cog, in My invitations, and the bell tells you when a new one arrives.

Signing in later

  1. Go to your firm's AuditISA address. Save it as a bookmark the first time.
  2. Type your email address and your password.
  3. Click Log in.

To leave, click Sign out at the right of the top bar. Do this every time on a shared or public computer.

Careful If you see a band reading STAGING - test data, no email at the bottom of the screen, you are on a test copy, not on your firm's real tool. Nothing you enter there counts.

If you forget your password

The sign-in page shows Forgot your password? under the password box only when your firm's email sending is switched on. The tool then emails you a link to choose a new password.

  1. On the sign-in page, click Forgot your password?.
  2. Enter your email address and confirm.
  3. Open the email from AuditISA and follow its link. The page says Welcome to and the name of your firm, then Choose a new password for your account.
  4. Type the new password twice, then click Save my new password.

If you do not see Forgot your password?, your firm's email sending is off. Ask your firm administrator: they give you a New password link from the Users screen. It opens the same page, works once and expires after 24 hours.
