The completion checklist and issuing the report

The completion checklist shows whether the engagement is ready, and is where the engagement partner signs off and issues the report.

Who: the team answers the questions. Only the engagement partner signs the checklist and issues the report.

The completion checklist

Open C6.1 and click Completion checklist. The page has two parts:

  • 1 · Checked by the tool: checks that AuditISA runs on the engagement by itself: every task addressed, every working paper signed and reviewed, every review note cleared, controls and substantive procedures concluded, and significant deficiencies communicated. Each check is green or red, and a red one names the tasks concerned.
  • 2 · Confirmed by the team — a note is required for each answer: thirteen questions, for example "Subsequent events reviewed up to the report date" and "Independence re-confirmed by every team member". Answer Yes or No and write a note. Only a Yes with its note turns green.

Issuing the report

  1. When every line is green, the engagement partner clicks Partner sign-off: ready to issue.
  2. The partner then clicks Issue the report, which appears on the same page once the partner has signed, and confirms. The report date is recorded.
Careful If an answer changes after the partner has signed, the sign-off is voided. The partner signs again before the report is issued.
