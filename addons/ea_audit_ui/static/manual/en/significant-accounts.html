Significant accounts

Decide, lead index by lead index, which accounts are significant and their relevant assertions.

Who: the engagement team.

Significant accounts (P6.2)

The Significant accounts page has one row per lead index (for example UA Revenue). For each row you see the Current year, the Prior year, the Variance and Variance %, whether the balance is Above threshold, and, when the general ledger is imported, how many Transactions, Preparers and Reviewers it holds.

  1. At the top, set Flag a significant movement: a percentage and an amount in FCFA, joined by and (both limits passed) or or (either one). The defaults are 10% and 10,000,000 FCFA with and. Flagged rows are counted next to a flag icon. A new account with no prior-year balance counts as above the percentage.
  2. In the Risks column, pick from the engagement's risk register the risks that bear on the account. Any live risk linked locks the row at Significant.
  3. Check the Status the tool proposes (Significant or Not significant). Change it in the cell if your judgement differs.
  4. When you depart from the proposed status, write the Reason. A departure without a reason shows in red.
  5. Tick the relevant assertions in the Relevant assertions column for every significant account.
Good to know The presumed risk of fraud in revenue is linked to revenue (UA) automatically, so revenue stays significant until a partner approves a rebuttal in the risk register. An account excluded here is not worked later, so document an exclusion as carefully as an inclusion.

Use Export to Excel and Import from Excel to work offline. Carried from shows decisions brought over from last year's engagement.
