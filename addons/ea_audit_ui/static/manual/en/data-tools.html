The Data bank

Keep the client's files in one place: the trial balance, the ledgers and the registers the tools work on.

Who: the engagement team. Roles that only read (for example the quality reviewer) see the pages in read-only mode.

The tiles

The Data bank tile opens one tile per kind of file. Each tile says in one line what the file holds, and shows the files uploaded (how many, and for which timings: prior year, pre-audit, post-audit, subsequent period), or "Not uploaded". Click a tile to see its uploads, with Import a dataset (Import a trial balance for the trial balance) and Download the template:

TileWhat it holds
Trial balanceBalances of every account, opening and closing.
General ledgerEvery journal entry of the year.
Accounts receivable ledgerOpen items by customer.
Accounts payable ledgerOpen items by supplier.
Inventory ledgerItems in stock, quantities and values.
Fixed assets registerEach asset, its cost and depreciation.
Payroll sub-ledgerPay by employee and month.

The Data bank holds uploads only. The analyses of the general ledger, the financial analysis and the analytical procedures are in EAGLA.

Data quality (IPE)

Data quality (IPE) checks every file the client gives us as soon as it is uploaded, records where the data comes from, and holds the team's conclusion on whether the working papers can rely on it. See Data quality (IPE).
