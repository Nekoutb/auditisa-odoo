Classes of transactions, what can go wrong and controls

Open Transactions and controls (S1.1 to S2.2). The page shows one card per class of transactions (sales, purchases, payroll and so on): green when complete, amber to finish, red not started. A warning lists any significant account not yet covered by a class.

  1. Click New class of transactions. Give its name, type (Routine, Non-routine or Estimation), the IT applications it uses (separate them with commas) and its Strategy: Controls or Substantive.
  2. Click Assign the class to give it to a team member.
  3. Tab 1 Lead indexes: click Link a lead index and tick the assertions.
  4. Tab 2 What can go wrong: click Add what can go wrong for each risk in the flow, with its assertions.
  5. Tab 3 Controls: click Add a control. Record its owner, type (Manual, IT-dependent manual, Automated), frequency, Reliance (Normal or Heavy / significant risk), design and whether it is implemented. Tick To test for each control you will test. The grid Which control answers which risk links controls to risks: click a cell to link or unlink.
  6. Tab 4 Walkthrough: describe the flow (Initiation, Recording, Processing, Reporting, people, documents, systems), then the walkthrough itself, from Transaction selected to the conclusion.

The panel beside the tabs holds the documents of the class (flowcharts, procedure manuals).
