Work allocation

Choose who prepares and who reviews each task of the engagement, on one page. Tasks that need the partner's approval go to the engagement partner automatically.

Who: the engagement partner and the managers on the team change the allocation. The firm administrator can too, when on the team. Everyone else on the team can read it.

Opening the page

  1. Open the engagement.
  2. Click the tools icon (the four squares, Engagement tools) in the top bar.
  3. Click the Work allocation tile.

What the page shows

The tasks are grouped by phase: Planning, Strategy and risks, Execution and Conclusion. Each group says how many tasks it holds and how many are not allocated yet. Each task has one row:

ColumnWhat it says
TaskThe task's code and title.
Rule from the sign-off policyWho may prepare and who may review, in plain words. For example: “Prepared by a senior or above; partner approves”. The firm's sign-off policy sets this rule.
Preparer and ReviewerPick a person in each list. The lists show only the accepted members of the engagement team, each with their role.
Partner approvalWhen the rule asks for the partner's approval, the engagement partner's name, marked automatic. You do not choose it. Otherwise Not needed.
DeadlineThe task's deadline.
StatusWhere the task stands: not started, in progress, prepared, reviewed or signed.

The filters across the top show All tasks, the tasks Not allocated, the tasks waiting for the partner's approval (Needs partner approval), or only yours (Mine). Each filter shows how many tasks it holds.

Allocating the tasks

  1. For each task, choose the Preparer and the Reviewer.
  2. To give several tasks to the same people, tick their rows. A bar appears: choose the Preparer and the Reviewer, then click Apply to the selection.
  3. To fill the empty boxes in one go, click Suggest by role. The preparer is the most junior team member the rule allows; the reviewer is the next level up, never the preparer. The tasks are spread evenly. Boxes already filled are not changed.
  4. Click Save. Nothing is saved before you click it.

Red lines under a box tell you what is wrong:

MessageWhat happens
The reviewer cannot be the preparer.The page cannot be saved. Nobody reviews their own work: choose another reviewer.
Below the minimum role (for example, a senior or above must prepare this task)A warning only. You can save. When the person signs, the sign-off policy still applies, and it refuses a signature below the minimum role.

Export to Excel gives you the allocation as saved, one row per task.

Workload

The Workload panel on the right lists each team member with their role, how many tasks they have to prepare and to review, and a small bar. For the engagement partner, it also counts the tasks they approve automatically. Click a name to see only that person's tasks; click it again to see all of them.

Partner approval

Partner approval needs no choice. Every task whose rule asks for the partner's approval goes to the engagement partner by itself: when the engagement opens, when a task comes onto the file later, and when the partner role is transferred to someone else (Transfer the partner role).

Good to know The lists show only the engagement team. Changing an allocation never removes signatures already given; the new person signs from now on. Every change is recorded in the activity trail, and the person named gets a notification.
