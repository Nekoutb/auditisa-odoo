Materiality

Set overall materiality, performance materiality and the clearly trivial threshold, and keep every version with its approval.

Who: the engagement team prepares a version; a partner approves it.

Setting materiality (P6.1)

The Materiality page shows three cards: Overall materiality, Performance materiality and Clearly trivial misstatements. Below them you see the Trial balance bases (the possible benchmarks, worked out from the trial balance), your justification, and the Version history.

  1. Click Generate a version.
  2. Choose the benchmark (Profit before tax, Revenue, Total assets, Equity or Total expenses), check the Base amount and type the Percentage. If last year's engagement exists, a blue line reminds you of Last year's basis.
  3. Check Performance % (75 by default) and Clearly trivial % (5 by default). If you change the performance percentage, explain why.
  4. Write the Justification. It is required: the tool never fills in a stock sentence for you.
  5. If your percentage is outside the usual range for that benchmark, or your base amount does not match the trial balance, fill Why it departs from the range or the trial balance base.
  6. Click Generate. The new version is saved as a draft.

Approval

A partner then clicks Approve the latest version. Only an approved version counts: until the partner approves a new draft, the earlier approved version stays in force, and a yellow banner says so. Approved materiality feeds P6.1, P6.2 and the summary of audit differences (C1.1). Click Export to Excel for a workbook copy.

Good to know If you revise materiality later, the new version needs approval again, and the trail notes that work done under the earlier figure needs review.
