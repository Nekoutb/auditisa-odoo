Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions auditors ask most often.

Who: everyone.

My review signature has disappeared. Why?

Someone changed the working paper after you signed: an answer, or a file attached or removed. Your signature covered the earlier version, so it was voided. You will find it under Voided signatures with the date and reason. Review the change and sign again. See Signing a working paper.

Why can't I sign as reviewer?

The most common reasons: you signed the same working paper as preparer (no self-review), review notes are still open, the preparer has not signed yet, or your role is below the minimum in your firm's sign-off policy. The message on screen tells you which one applies.

I clicked "No exceptions noted" but it is greyed out.

A tool has raised a warning on this working paper, so it must be documented in full. Describe the work and the result in the text box instead.

Why does my working paper show a text box per task when the client is non-complex?

The working paper went back to full documentation because a tool raised a warning, a conclusion statement is answered No, or the working paper is one that is never lightened. A line under the objective tells you which.

I lost my changes.

Nothing is recorded until you click Save the paper. Watch for Unsaved changes next to the button. If you try to leave a working paper with unsaved changes, AuditISA asks you first.

Two of us edited the same working paper at the same time.

Each person's changes are kept where they do not overlap. If you both changed the same box, your text stays and your colleague's version is shown underneath with Use their version.

Who can clear my review note?

Only these people may clear a note: you, as its author, or a manager or above who did not prepare the working paper. Anyone on the team can reply. See Review notes.

How do I correct an adjustment that is already posted?

A reviewer clicks Unpost on the summary of audit differences. Change the adjustment on its working paper, then have it posted again. The unposting is logged.

Can I unarchive an engagement file?

No. An archived engagement file stays sealed and read-only. To add something, use Add an addendum on the archive page. See Archiving the engagement file.

The Roll forward button refuses to start.

An engagement can be rolled forward only once it is closed: its report issued or its engagement file archived. You also need to be a manager or above on the team.

A file in the new year is marked "Prior year, carried forward".

It was copied from last year. Check that it still holds, then click Mark as reviewed. See Rolling forward to next year.
