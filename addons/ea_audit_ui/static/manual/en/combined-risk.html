Who: the engagement team.

The combined risk assessment (S3.1)

The Combined risk assessment (S3.1) lists only the significant accounts and only their relevant assertions. If the page is empty, it tells you what is missing: a valid trial balance, approved materiality, or accounts marked significant.

  1. For each assertion, choose Inherent risk (Lower or Higher) and Control risk (Rely or Not rely). The tool shows its suggestion and works out the Combined level (Minimal, Low, Moderate, High) and the sampling level.
  2. When you differ from the suggestion, write the Basis / reason.
  3. Tick Show only what is still to assess to see what is left.

Each click saves at once: there is no Save button. Significant risks in the register that are linked to no account are listed at the bottom so none is forgotten.

Careful Rely needs a tested control covering that assertion and IT general controls that support reliance, or a written control risk basis. Once planning is closed, changing an assessment asks for a reason (E6.8).
