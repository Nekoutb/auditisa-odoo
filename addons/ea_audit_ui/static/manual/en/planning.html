The Planning phase

What the Planning phase holds, the tools that do the figures for you, and the order in which to work so that each step has what it needs.

Who: the whole engagement team prepares; a partner approves materiality.

What the Planning phase holds

Inside an engagement, click Planning in the top bar. The dashboard shows the ring Planning and Risk Identification with its percentage done. Click the ring to see its groups of tasks (the working papers whose codes start with P) and how many are finished in each.

Some working papers only appear when something on the engagement calls for them (for example P2.3 for a group audit).

Each working paper has a Tools panel on the right. Its buttons open the tool that does the work for that working paper. When a tool already holds the answer, the step on the working paper says from the tool and is filled for you. You still write your own conclusion on the working paper.

The order to work in

The planning tools feed each other. Follow this order and nothing will be empty when you open it:

  1. Import and check the trial balance of the year (and the prior year). Without a valid trial balance, the tools below have no figures.
  2. Look at the Financial analysis and the Analytical procedures (P3.2) to spot unusual movements.
  3. Set and get approval for Materiality (P6.1).
  4. Decide the Significant accounts and their relevant assertions (P6.2). This uses the approved materiality.
  5. Move on to Strategy and risks: the combined risk assessment only lists the accounts marked significant.

You can open every tool from the working paper's Tools panel, or from the Engagement tools button (the grid icon in the top bar), which shows all the tools of the engagement as tiles.
