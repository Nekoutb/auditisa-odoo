Your home page

Your home page lists the engagements you work on and what is waiting for you.

Who: everyone in the firm.

What your home page shows

When you sign in, AuditISA opens your home page, the Dashboard. It greets you by name (Welcome) and lists Your engagements. You only see the engagements whose team you belong to.

  • An invitation to join an engagement team shows as a large card at the top of the page: You are invited to … as …, with the engagement and your role. Click Accept: the invitation opens and you accept it there (see Answering an invitation). Click Decline to say no and give a reason. Accepting means you confirm that you are independent of this client for this year.
  • The To do card gathers what needs you across all your engagements: Working papers awaiting review, Acceptance awaiting the partner's sign-off, Overdue tasks and Significant deficiency to communicate, with the engagement each line belongs to. Click a line to go straight there.
  • One card per engagement: the client, the period end and the kind of engagement; the current phase and its Phase deadline; Report due by; the engagement partner; and whether the acceptance is signed (Acceptance signed or Acceptance to sign).
  • A bar and a percentage show how far the engagement file has got. Click Continue to open the engagement.
  • An archived engagement shows Archived on a date and File locked. Click Open to read it; nothing in it can be changed.

While you are on no team yet, the page says You are in the firm. Your engagements appear here once a manager adds you to a team and you accept. A partner, a manager or the firm administrator also reads Start with + New engagement. The + New engagement button appears for everyone except read-only users.

Tip To jump between engagements, use the ENGAGEMENTS button in the top bar (Recent engagements, All engagements). The Search… box finds engagements and tasks, and also searches the methodology guidance (In the methodology) and your team's answers (In the answers).
