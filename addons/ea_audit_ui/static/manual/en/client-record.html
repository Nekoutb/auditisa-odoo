The client record

What each field of a client record is for, and how to keep the record up to date from one year to the next.

Who: anyone in the firm can read a client record. Only a partner or the firm administrator can change an existing one.

What each field is for

FieldWhat to enter, and why it matters
NameThe legal name. It appears on the dashboard, the reports and the letters.
Legal FormSA (board of directors), SA (administrateur général), SARL, SAS, GIE or Other. Some legal tasks depend on it: for example, one of the OHADA legal procedures does not apply to an SARL or a GIE.
SectorThe client's main activity (trade, services, financial, energy and so on).
Reporting frameworkRevised SYSCOHADA, normal system. It is the only framework offered today and is filled in for you. A new engagement takes it from the client.
Governance ModeHow the entity is run: a board with a combined chairman and managing director, a board with a separate managing director, a sole director, or management by gérant(s).
RegulatorThe sector regulator, if any (for example COBAC for banks or CIMA for insurers).
Registration Number and NIUThe trade register number and the tax identification number. Both can be searched.
AddressThe registered office address.
Share capital (FCFA)Used by the check of equity against half of the share capital in the OHADA legal work. If you leave it empty, the tool looks for the capital in the trial balance.
Mandate Type and Mandate Start YearWhether you were appointed by the statutes (2 financial years) or by the general meeting (6 financial years), and the first year. They let the tool work out when your mandate ends.
Listed, Joint auditor (co-CAC), Public interest entityTick the boxes that apply. They record the client's status. Listed and Joint auditor (co-CAC) are also used when the audit report is issued: for a listed client, key audit matters are required.
Good to know Ticking Listed or Public interest entity here does not set how complex the engagement is. That is decided only by the answers to the engagement questionnaire (see The engagement questionnaire).

Keeping the record up to date

Review the client record at the start of each year, before you open the new engagement. Changes of share capital, governance or regulator are common and some tasks rely on them.

  1. Open the client from the Clients list.
  2. Change the fields you need. Only a partner or the firm administrator can save a change.
  3. Click the save icon.
Good to know If you change the client's name, the new name is used everywhere at once, including on past engagements.
