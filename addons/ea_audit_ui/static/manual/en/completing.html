Completing the audit

The Conclusion phase holds the final reviews, the representation letter, the reports and the engagement partner's approval.

Who: the whole team. The quality reviewer completes C4.2. The engagement partner approves on C4.1.

Click Conclusion in the top bar. The tasks are grouped as follows:

Which tasks you see depends on the client's legal form and on the engagement profile. For example, C5.7 does not apply to an SARL. Every working paper works as described in Working on a working paper.

Subsequent events and going concern (C2.2)

C2.2 brings your going concern work to its final conclusion. It starts from the preliminary view in S4.2 and the execution work in E6.3. Carry your procedures up to the report date, make a final inquiry of management, read the minutes since your last reading, and decide whether each event requires an adjustment or a disclosure. Then conclude whether the going concern basis is appropriate and whether a material uncertainty exists. The card at the top repeats the equity figures from the trial balance.

The representation letter (C3.1)

  1. Open Templates in the Tools box to start from the firm's letter.
  2. Add the representations specific to this engagement and the materiality used in the letter.
  3. Have the partner review the draft before it goes to the client.
  4. Attach the signed letter. It must be dated no later than the auditor's report.

C3.1 is always in full documentation, whatever the profile.

Reports and communications

C5.1 Opinion and report records the opinion, the components of the report, going concern, key audit matters, any emphasis or other matter, and the report date. Its tools are Summary of audit differences, Materiality and Findings. The other statutory reports (C5.3, C5.4, C5.10) cover the OHADA special reports. C5.11 records what you told those charged with governance and management, including the control deficiencies from the Findings tool.

Quality review (C4.2)

Where an engagement quality review is required, the quality reviewer evaluates the team's significant judgements. They raise review notes on the working papers and record their review on C4.2. Only the appointed quality reviewer completes and signs C4.2. The review must be complete before the report is dated.

Partner review and approval (C4.1, C4.3)

On C4.3 Points Outstanding, list what is still open and clear each point with evidence. On C4.1 Review and Approval, the engagement partner confirms that every planned procedure was performed and every working paper is signed. Every point outstanding must be cleared and no review note left open. The misstatements must be evaluated, the quality review complete, and the evidence must support the opinion. Its Tools box links to Review notes, Audit programs and Team.

Work after the report date

After the report date, only assembly work is allowed. A new finding, misstatement or procedure conclusion is refused unless a manager or above clicks Open, with this reason under Work after the report date and gives the reason. Each item recorded is then named in the activity trail. Close the work after the report date ends it.
