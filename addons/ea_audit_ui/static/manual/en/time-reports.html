Time reports

Follow the hours recorded, by person, by engagement or by week.

Who: the firm administrator, partners and managers see the whole firm; a quality reviewer sees the engagements they review; everyone else sees their own hours.

Reading the time reports

Click Reports on the Timesheet page. The line under Time reports tells you what you can see:

Your roleWhat the report covers
Firm administrator, partner, managerThe whole firm
Quality reviewer on an engagementYour hours and the team's on the engagements you review
Everyone elseYour hours
  1. Type the period in From and To (DD/MM/YYYY) and click Show.
  2. Choose By person, By engagement or By week.
  3. For your own analysis, click Pivot table, or Excel export to download the figures with every line in detail.
