Rolling forward to next year

You can start next year's engagement from a closed one, meaning its report is issued or its engagement file is archived.

  1. Open the engagement record of last year's engagement and click Roll forward.
  2. Under 1 · What to carry, untick anything you do not want to carry. Everything is ticked by default.
  3. Under 2 · Year, team and process, check the Year end and the Engagement partner. Choose the Approach: Same as last year keeps last year's tasks, and Change reopens the profile questionnaire.
  4. Review the Team. Last year's members are listed. Tick or untick Invite, change a Role, or add a line with an email and role for a new member. People who have left the firm are not carried.
  5. Click Create the file.
Carried (as drafts to update or re-confirm)How it arrives
Tasks and scope (profile answers)The questionnaire must be concluded again.
Materiality basis and percentagesNo amounts: they are worked out from this year's figures.
Classes of transactions, controls and walkthrough notesEvery test result, conclusion and sign-off starts again.
Risk register, procedure design, significance decisionsAs drafts, each one to re-confirm. Procedures are not started.
Prior-year balancesLast year's closing trial balance becomes the comparatives (ISA 510).
Working paper answersMarked "Carried forward from (year): to update".
All of last year's evidenceCopied and marked "Prior year, carried forward: review before relying on it". Click Mark as reviewed once you have checked a file.
Good to know Sign-offs, conclusions and independence always start again in the new engagement. Review notes, completion records, the done or not-applicable ticks of procedures, and the answers of C4.1, C4.2, C6.1 and C6.2 are not carried either.
