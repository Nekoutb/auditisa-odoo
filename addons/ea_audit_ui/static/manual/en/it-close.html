IT applications and the close process

Record each IT application with its strategy and conclusion, and document the financial statement close process.

Who: the engagement team.

IT applications and IT controls

IT applications and IT controls (S2.3 to S2.5) lists every application named on your classes of transactions. For each, record its Layers (application, database, operating system), a strategy and a note:

  • Rely on the IT processes: test the IT general controls
  • Test the automated controls directly, each period
  • Fully substantive: no reliance on the IT controls

When the work is done, click Record the S2.5 conclusion: Support reliance, Do not support reliance or Mixed. This conclusion feeds the combined risk assessment.

The close process

Close process (S1.4) is a form in four parts: A. The critical path of the close, B. What can go wrong, C. Changes and communications, D. Strategy and confirmation.
