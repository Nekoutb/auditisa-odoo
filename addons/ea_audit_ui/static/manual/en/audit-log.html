Audit log

Every change made in the firm's engagement files, with who made it and when.

Who: the firm administrator only.

Reading the log

Click the cog (Settings), then Audit log. Each line shows the Date, the User, the Engagement, the Task, a Summary, and for an answer the full text Before and After. Nobody can edit or delete it. Filter on Answer changes or Firm settings, or group by user, engagement or task.
