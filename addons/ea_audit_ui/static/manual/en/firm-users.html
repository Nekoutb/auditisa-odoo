Users of the firm

Bring your colleagues into AuditISA, give each the right role, and stop someone's access when needed.

Who: the firm administrator. Partners see some of the firm settings too (see the table below).

Where the firm settings are

Click the cog (Settings) in the top bar. What you see depends on your role:

Item under the cogWhat it is forWho sees it
Sign-off policyThe firm-wide sign-off rulesPartners and the firm administrator
Sign-off matrixWho prepares, reviews and signs each taskPartners and the firm administrator
File indexThe list of every task an engagement file can holdPartners and the firm administrator
Audit logEvery change made in the firm's engagement filesFirm administrator only
UsersInvite people, set roles, deactivateFirm administrator only

Users and Settings are also in the Apps grid (nine dots).

Adding a team member to the firm

  1. Open Users.
  2. In the top box, type the person's Email and Name, and choose their Role (see the table below).
  3. Click Invite. A green box appears with the Invitation link.
  4. Click Copy and send the link to the person yourself, by email or a private message. They choose their own password: none is created or sent. The link expires after 6 days.

The person appears in the list as Invited, then Active once they have signed in, with their Last login. If their link expired or was lost, click New link on their row (only possible until they first sign in). To change a role, choose another one in their row. To stop someone's access, click Deactivate; Reactivate undoes it. Nothing they did is deleted.

RoleWhat the person can do
Firm administratorEverything a partner can, plus users, firm settings, the audit log and the analytics thresholds.
PartnerCan be the engagement partner of an engagement and give partner sign-offs; sets the sign-off rules; sees the whole firm's time reports.
ManagerRuns engagements, reviews work, sets time budgets; sees the whole firm's time reports.
SeniorPrepares work and can review the work of others, depending on your firm's sign-off policy.
StaffPrepares working papers.
Engagement quality reviewerReviews the engagements they are asked to review and signs the quality review; does not change the engagement file.
Read onlyReads the engagement files of their teams; changes nothing.
Good to know A role gives no access to an engagement: only the engagement's team does. After inviting someone to the firm, the engagement partner or manager still adds them to each engagement team.
Careful Some changes are refused, to protect the firm: you cannot change your own access; the firm must keep at least one active administrator; and a person who is the engagement partner of an engagement cannot be deactivated or moved below partner until that role is transferred.

Further down the Users screen:

  • Allowed email domains: one per line (for example your firm's own domain). Only addresses in these domains can be invited. Empty means no restriction. Click Save.
  • Administration log: who invited whom, role changes, new links, deactivations. A row marked Technical support shows when AuditISA support entered the firm.
