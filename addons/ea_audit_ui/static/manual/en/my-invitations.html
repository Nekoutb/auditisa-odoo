Answering an invitation

Accept or decline an invitation to join an engagement team.

Who: any member of the firm who has been invited to an engagement team.

Answering an invitation

  1. On your home page, click Accept on the invitation card (You are invited to … as …): the invitation opens. You can also click the gear icon (Settings), then My invitations, and open the invitation, or click it in the bell.
  2. The invitation It shows the Engagement name, the Client, the Fiscal year and your role.
  3. Click Accept, or click Decline and give a Reason.
Careful By accepting, you confirm that you are independent of this client for this year. If you have any doubt about your independence (a family link, a financial interest, a past role at the client), speak to the engagement partner before you accept.

Until you accept, you cannot open the engagement. A decline and its reason are written to the engagement's activity trail, so the partner knows why.

Good to know The independence conclusion for the whole engagement is documented on task P2.1. The firm's yearly independence declarations are collected outside AuditISA.
